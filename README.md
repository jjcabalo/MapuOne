### 🔴 The Platform

**MapuOne** is a dynamic, centralized hub designed to revolutionize the way concerns are raised, tracked, and resolved. In an environment where transparency and accountability matter most, MapuOne bridges the gap between individuals and administrators, ensuring that every filed complaint is treated with urgency, structure, and absolute confidentiality.
